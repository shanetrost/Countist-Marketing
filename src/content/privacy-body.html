Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-09-15

1. Who we are

Countist is a product of Fylum Agency LLC, a New York limited liability company. In this policy, "Countist," "we," "us," and "our" mean Fylum Agency LLC operating Countist.

Questions about this policy, or a request about your data, go to privacy@fylum.agency.

2. What this policy covers

This one policy covers everything Countist: the marketing site at countist.app, the web application at app.countist.app, and the Countist app for iOS. Where something applies to only one of those, it says so.

3. What we collect

Account information. When you create an account we collect your email address and any profile information you provide through Clerk, our authentication provider. We do not store passwords. If you sign in with Google, we receive your email address and name from Google and nothing else.

Your subscription records. Countist is a manual-entry tool. Every subscription record you create, the service name, price, renewal date and notes, is stored in our database and belongs to you. We never connect to your bank, your credit card, or any financial account. That is the core design choice of the product, not a limitation.

Payment information. If you subscribe to a paid plan, Stripe processes the payment. We receive confirmation that a payment succeeded, your subscription status, and the last four digits and brand of your card so we can show you which card is on file. We never see or store your full card number or security code.

Usage data on the marketing site. We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how people find and use countist.app. It collects pages viewed, time on page, referring source, browser and device type, and approximate location derived from your IP address at the city or region level.

In-app notifications. We use Knock to deliver notifications inside the app. It processes your user identifier and the notification content.

AI features, and exactly what leaves Countist. Countist uses AI to check subscription prices, spot overlapping services and offer savings tips. Those features are powered by Anthropic's Claude API. Here is precisely what we send.

Price check. We send only the name of the subscription service, for example "Netflix". We do not send the price you entered, your renewal date, or anything about your account. The comparison against your price happens on our own systems. Because the answer is public pricing information rather than your data, we cache it and reuse it for anyone who looks up the same service.

Spending insights, on paid plans. We send a category-level summary of your subscriptions: which categories you have subscriptions in, how many are in each, the monthly total for each category, and your overall monthly total. For example, "Entertainment: 3 subscriptions, $47 per month."

We do not send your name, email address, account identifier, individual subscription names, renewal dates or payment details. This summary does not identify you by name, but it does describe your subscription spending. Spending insights are a paid-plan feature, so if you would rather this never leave Countist, do not use them. Free accounts never send it.

Under Anthropic's commercial terms, Anthropic does not use data submitted through its API to train its models.

Error monitoring. We use Sentry to record errors and crashes so we can fix them. An error report can include the page you were on, your browser and device, and an identifier for your account.

Email. If you opt in to product updates, Mailchimp stores your email address and sends them. Transactional email about your account and trial is separate and is sent whether or not you opt in to marketing.

Server and network logs. Our hosting and CDN providers automatically log requests, including IP address, timestamp and user agent, for security and reliability.

4. What we do not collect

We do not connect to financial accounts. We do not collect your name unless you choose to enter it, your phone number, your mailing address, your full card details, or your transaction history from any bank or card issuer. We do not buy personal data about you from third parties. We do not track you across other websites.

5. How we use what we collect

To run your account and show you your subscription records. To detect duplicate and overlapping services and warn you about them. To send renewal reminders and trial notices. To process payments. To improve the product and the marketing site. To keep the service secure.

We do not use your subscription records to build a profile of you, and we do not use them for advertising.

6. We do not sell or share your personal information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, as those terms are defined under California law. We run no advertising pixels and no retargeting on any Countist surface.

8a. How we protect it

In transit. Every connection between your browser or the iOS app and Countist is encrypted with TLS. Connections between Countist and its database are encrypted using TLS 1.2 or 1.3.

At rest. Your subscription records are stored in Neon's PostgreSQL infrastructure, which encrypts data at rest using AES-256.

Access. Access to production systems is limited to authorized personnel. We never store your password, because Clerk handles authentication. We never store your full card number, because Stripe handles payments.

No system is perfectly secure and we will not claim otherwise. If we ever become aware of a breach affecting your data, we will tell you.

10. Your rights

Wherever you live, you can:

  • see what personal information we hold about you
  • export your subscription data at any time from your account settings
  • correct your profile information
  • delete your account and all associated data from your account settings
  • unsubscribe from marketing email using the link in any message

Account export and deletion are self-service and immediate. For anything else, email privacy@fylum.agency and we will respond within 45 days. We will not treat you differently, or degrade the service, because you made a request.

If you live in California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Texas, Oregon, or another state with a comprehensive privacy law, you have these rights by statute, plus the right to appeal if we refuse a request. To appeal, reply to our response and say you are appealing.

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you also have the right to object to processing, the right to data portability, and the right to complain to your national data protection authority.

11. Children

Countist is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has created an account, email privacy@fylum.agency and we will delete it.

12. Where your data is handled

We operate from the United States and our providers process data in the United States. If you use Countist from outside the US, your information is transferred here.
